# DeployGuard Active Implementation Queue

Status: Iteration 0 baseline, 2026-08-01  
Authority: this is the sole active queue. The intended product is defined only by
`RAILWAY_PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md`; other trackers/reports are historical evidence.

## Protected baseline — do not redesign

Shared normal-v1 release, infrastructure-plan, and infrastructure-apply workers;
shared/canary activation; immutable intents/manifests; deterministic outbox and
BullMQ identities; advisory ownership; leases/fencing/heartbeat; journals;
bounded reconciliation; isolation; stable/latest separation; unchanged-source
`NO_OP`; and truthful durable history are verified foundations.

The local baseline has three running Docker-healthy shared `dev` worker roles and
exactly three live capability rows with role/runtime metadata and no project
binding. Release and apply report `ready=true`; plan remains live but reports the
truthful terminal result of an independently initiated rejected job and therefore
`ready=false`. Active operation leases, cross-lane owners, and outbox claims are
zero. PostgreSQL, Redis, backend, and frontend are live. Stripe and SNS remain
disabled, deferred non-blocking productization scope.

## Iteration 0 observations

- The shared engine baseline is locally healthy, but it is not by itself a
  Railway-like product launch verdict.
- During read-only observation an independently initiated disposable Express
  project produced one infrastructure intent that failed safely with
  `REMOTE_CANARY_PLAN_NOT_ALLOWED`; its outbox was historically published once,
  one failure journal exists, and no active lease/owner/claim remains. The plan
  worker process/capability remains live and Docker-healthy, while its sanitized
  status is terminal and not ready. Iteration 0 did not create, retry, clean, or
  mutate that evidence.
- Production release and infrastructure BullMQ queues have zero active, waiting,
  paused, waiting-child, delayed, or prioritized jobs. Historical job keys were
  preserved rather than treated as active residue.
- Full adapter acceptance for the seven candidate stacks remains unproven; the
  product contract therefore labels them candidates rather than launch-supported.

## Deferred, non-blocking scope

Stripe billing provider delivery, SNS notification delivery, broader optional
security/provider integrations, and live AWS deployment mutation are outside this
transformation's P0/P1 local launch acceptance. Their gates remain disabled and
their absence must not be represented as product evidence.
